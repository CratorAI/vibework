- **£184m** revenue, up 9% on FY2025
- **6.8%** operating margin, up from 4.1%
- **97.2%** on-time delivery, the best in five years

Source: Northwind management accounts, FY2026 (unaudited)

---

<!-- layout: bullets -->
# Three decisions drove the recovery

1. **Route consolidation.** Fourteen depots merged into nine; empty miles down 22%.
2. **Fuel hedging.** 70% of diesel locked at £1.18 a litre through FY27.
3. **Pricing discipline.** Exited 31 contracts earning below 3% margin.

Notes:
Each of these was a board decision in FY25. Consolidation was the hardest: two depot closures, 140 roles moved, 38 redundancies.

# Leeds would put 80% of customers within four hours

The site sits at the junction of the M1 and M62, next to our two largest grocery customers.

- **Today:** 61% of delivery points within four hours
- **With Leeds:** 80%, and 12% fewer trunk miles
- **Cost:** £38m over two years, payback in year five

![High-bay racking in a distribution centre aisle](images/hub.jpg)

Notes:
Drive-time analysis uses FY26 delivery points weighted by volume. Payback assumes 85% utilisation by year three.

| Option | Capex | Annual saving | Payback | Ready |

| Expand Daventry | £14.2m | £2.7m | 5.3 years | Q4 2027 |
| Do nothing | — | — | — | — |

Source: Strategy team analysis; capex quotes from two contractors (October 2026)

Notes:
